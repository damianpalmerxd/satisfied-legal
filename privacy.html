Privacy Policy

Satisfied · Effective August 10, 2026

Satisfied is built so there is very little about you to protect. There are no accounts. We store nothing about you on our servers. Your pantry, your saved combos, your answers, and your conversations with the coach live on your phone and nowhere else.

What stays on your phone

All of it, unless it is listed in the next section. Specifically: your pantry list, your saved combos, the answers you give when the app asks whether a meal held you, your coach conversation, your onboarding choices, and whether you subscribe. This information is written to your device's standard app storage. We cannot see it. If you delete the app, it is gone.

What leaves your phone, and why

To answer a question about a meal, the app has to send that question somewhere. Each time you ask for a read, these things are sent to our service and passed to Google's Gemini model, which generates the answer:

  • The photo you took, if you took one — of a meal, a menu, or a grocery receipt.
  • The text you typed, if you typed instead — a meal description, a craving, or a message to the coach.
  • Your pantry item names, so suggestions use food you actually have.
  • Your recent "did it hold you" answers — up to six, so the answers get more useful over time.
  • A recipe link, if you shared or pasted one, so we can read the page or the video's caption.

These are processed to produce your answer and then discarded. We do not save your photos on our servers. We do not save your messages. We do not keep a copy of anything you send. Our service holds no database of users, because there are no users to hold — nothing you send is tied to a name, an email address, or an account, because the app never asks for any.

The photo you keep

When you save a meal to your combos, the photo you took is kept — on your phone, in the app's own storage. It is not uploaded, not backed up to us, and not added to your camera roll. The copy that was sent for a read is still discarded; this one is simply yours. Deleting the combo deletes the photo with it, and deleting the app removes them all.

Receipt photos

When you scan a grocery receipt, the image is read once to pull out food item names, and only the names come back. Prices, totals, payment details, store loyalty numbers, and the image itself are never extracted and never stored.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind.
  • No weight, body measurements, or health data. The app does not connect to Apple Health or Health Connect, and does not ask to.
  • No calorie, macro, or nutrition numbers — the app does not calculate them for any purpose, including internally.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or third-party ad networks. There is no analytics software in this app.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, no microphone, no photo library scanning.

Who else is involved

  • Google (Gemini API) generates the answers. Your photo or text is sent to Google for processing, subject to Google's API terms. Google does not use paid Gemini API content to train its models.
  • Supabase hosts the small service that passes your request to Google. It stores none of it.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions. It receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase status — never your food, your messages, or your pantry.
  • Apple and Google process any payment. We never see your payment details.
  • Amazon, only if you tap a "get more" link to restock an item. That opens Amazon, and Amazon's own privacy practices apply from that point. We may earn a commission on purchases made through those links.

Camera

The app asks for camera access so you can photograph a meal, a menu, or a receipt. It is used for nothing else. You can decline and still use every feature by typing instead.

Children

Satisfied is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your control

Because your information lives on your device, you control it directly: clear your coach conversation from inside the app, remove pantry items or saved combos as you like, or delete the app to erase everything at once. There is no account to close and no data of yours for us to delete, because we are not holding any.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
